This is the first entry.

The writing here is meant to last. These are not bulletins or announcements. They are the slower thread of the work — the parts that are easier to say in sentences than in product changes.

Posts will be infrequent. Some will be edited after publication. A few will be retired when they stop being true.

The intent is to keep a record of the thinking behind the systems and worlds the studio builds, in a form that doesn't depend on a feed.

— FAF
